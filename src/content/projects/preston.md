Preston reads code, documentation, decisions, and conversations into a living map of engineering judgment. Coding agents consult that map before they work, and Preston reviews future changes against it.

Every conclusion keeps its justification. When a premise changes, Preston can revise what depended on it instead of quietly repeating stale context.
